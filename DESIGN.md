# Xpense Tracker — product design

Product promise: **a calm, local-first view of where your money goes.**

Lead with a monthly money story: available balance, uncategorized triage, category trend, and one next action. Use green for healthy, amber for attention, and red only for real overspend/data errors. Avoid decorative finance clichés.

Icon prompt: `compact ledger card with balanced rising bars and a small checkmark, calm personal finance control, flat vector, dark ink, cyan/green accents, no coins, no piggy bank, no text, transparent background`.

Diagram: transaction ingestion → parsing → category rules → monthly budget → trend charts → actionable insight.
